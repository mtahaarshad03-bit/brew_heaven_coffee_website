Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver?

Delivery is shown at checkout in this demo. Free delivery applies over Rs 3,000.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Yes. Choose pickup at checkout and select a café.

How do offer codes work?

Enter a code in your cart or apply it from the offers page. One code per order.

Do you take group reservations?

Tables seat up to 10 guests in this demo. Larger groups should use the contact form.

Do you have dairy-free options?

Oat and almond milk are available on every milk-based drink.

Can I change or cancel a reservation?

Cancel it from the reservations page, or contact the café.

Is my payment information safe?

This demo does not process payments or collect card details.
